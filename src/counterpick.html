      • ParryBlock just as a bullet arrives to send it back.
      • EdgesThe glowing edge hurts. Block as you hit it to bounce off safely.
      • WallsHold toward a wall to grip it, then jump off.
      • PlanksBright-edged planks are one-way. Press down to drop through. Pink pads launch you.
      • SwingsShoot hanging blocks to swing them into your rival.
      • CratesPush one for cover, or off a ledge onto someone. Three hits break it for a little health.
      • SupportsBright rock never moves; dark iron does. Shoot a glowing hollow support and what it holds swings, drops or topples.
