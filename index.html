How a match works
  1. Both players draft one card before the first shot.
  2. A point ends when one fighter dies. Two points win a round.
  3. The round's loser drafts one card from a hand of five. Cards stack.
  4. Win the set number of rounds to take the match.
  5. Online: one player hosts and shares a 4-letter code or link; the other joins with it.
  6. Survival: choose a character (more unlock as you reach new rounds), take one free pick (a card or a power-up), then fight waves of enemies that get tougher every round. Every 5th round is a boss (Sniper, Brute, Bombardier, Phantom or Warden, in a new order each run, and the Crown every 25th); it always warns you before its special. Some rounds bring a twist (low gravity, fast bullets...). After that first pick everything is bought with scrap (bosses pay extra). Damage earns XP: each level adds +5% health and +4% damage. Clearing a round pays scrap for the shop: cards and power-ups, lock one, reroll, or sell from your build. 3 lives for the whole run.
  7. Co-op Survival (Multiplayer): the same run with a friend. Each of you picks and shops on your own screen. If one of you goes down, they come back after 8, 12, then 16 seconds, or straight away if the other stands next to them for 2 seconds. Both down at once costs a life.
Controls
MOVEAD or ←→ move · W/Space/↑ jump · S/↓ duck, drop through planks, fall fast · mouse aims · click shoots · right-click or Shift blocks PHONEHold it sideways. Left thumb: a stick appears where you touch (run, flick up to jump, pull down to duck or drop). Right thumb: tap to auto-aim and fire, or drag to aim and let go to fire (drag back to the middle to cancel). Buttons for Jump and Block. Full screen is on the menu (on iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen). ALLBright-lipped planks are one-way. Pink pads launch you, and some platforms move. The glowing arena edge isn't a wall: touching it hurts and knocks you back, and falling to the bottom bounces you up with damage. Bullets fly right over it, so a high lob comes back down. Hold toward a wall to grip it, and jump off it. Tricks: block just as a bullet arrives to parry it back at the shooter; block as you hit the arena edge to launch off it unhurt; shoot a hanging block into your rival to smash them (a block knocks it back). Shoot hanging blocks to swing them. White orb in hand = block ready. Esc pause · M mute
